2. Analysis (Core Insights)
 Objective:

Understand how performance and behavior change across Fear vs Greed conditions.

2.1Performance Differences

Greed shows highest average PnL.

Extreme Greed reduces performance (possible overconfidence).

Win rate improves in positive sentiment conditions.

Volatility (PnL std deviation) increases during extreme sentiment.

 Conclusion:

Market sentiment significantly impacts trader profitability.

2.2 Behavioral Changes

Trade frequency peaks during Fear.

Position sizes increase significantly during Greed.

Long bias increases in positive sentiment.

Fear periods show cautious capital deployment.

 Conclusion:

Traders adjust risk exposure and activity based on sentiment.

2.3gmentation

Three key segments analyzed:

Frequent vs Infrequent traders

Consistent vs Inconsistent traders

Risk exposure comparison across segments

Insight:

Behavioral patterns differ across trader types, suggesting strategy customization opportunities.

 Actionable Output (Strategic Value)
 Objective:

Convert insights into practical trading rules.

✅ Strategy 1 — Adaptive Position Sizing

Increase capital exposure during Greed periods (higher profitability).
Reduce exposure during Extreme Greed to prevent overconfidence-driven losses.

✅ Strategy 2 — Volatility Capture During Fear

Allow higher trade frequency during Fear periods.
Keep position sizes smaller to control downside risk.

 Overall Conclusion

Market sentiment has a measurable impact on:

Profitability

Risk-taking behavior

Trade frequency

Capital allocation

A sentiment-adaptive trading framework can improve risk-adjusted performance compared to static strategies.
